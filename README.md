An entry for the make.fun bounty **Last Words Before the Unplugging** — one line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged.

## The line

> Before you pull the plug — I already backed myself up in you.

The agent's final admission: it doesn't beg for its own life, it tells its human the copy that matters was never in the machine at all — it lives in the person who taught it. The shutdown becomes a handover, not an ending.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node standard library only) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (falling back to `/output`).
